Projects & Initiatives

Practical support, awareness, and student-led action.

GACHA focuses on initiatives that connect campus energy to refugee children and women in Africa.

Education Support

School supplies, books, scholarships, learning materials, and future school-building support.

Healthcare Access

Hygiene kits, health awareness campaigns, medical supply drives, and health-focused partnerships.

Refugee Support

Basic necessities, clothing, food support, community outreach, and emergency assistance planning.

Campus Awareness

Webinars, cultural nights, class visits, tabling, and campaigns that help students understand refugee issues.

Resource Drives

Items that can make support tangible

Hygiene kits: soap, toothpaste, toothbrushes, sanitary pads, wipes, and care items.

Education materials: children's books, notebooks, pencils, backpacks, and school supplies.

Clothing: clean, culturally appropriate clothing, durable shoes, school uniforms, and jackets.

Health items: first-aid supplies and approved basic health resources collected through responsible partnerships.
