Why Africa?

Understanding the communities GACHA serves.

GACHA focuses on African refugee children and women because displacement can interrupt education, healthcare, safety, and family stability for years.

The Challenge

Displacement is more than leaving home.

For refugee families, displacement can mean losing access to school, medical care, legal protection, stable housing, and the networks that help people rebuild. Children may miss years of education, and women often carry heavy responsibilities for family safety, health, and survival.

Education barriers

School supplies, fees, transportation, language, trauma, and interrupted learning can keep children out of classrooms.

Healthcare access

Refugee communities may face limited clinics, medicine, maternal care, hygiene resources, and health information.

Basic needs

Food, clean water, clothing, safe shelter, and hygiene products are essential for stability and dignity.

Why students can help

Students can raise awareness, mobilize resources, build partnerships, and keep refugee stories visible.

A Personal Mission

Respectful, fact-based, and connected to lived experience.

GACHA's work is strengthened by students with personal connections to African communities and firsthand awareness of the challenges families can face. The goal is not to speak over refugees, but to listen, learn, partner responsibly, and help connect resources to real needs.
