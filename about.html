About Us

Students organizing for refugee children and women.

GACHA is the Global Africa Child Hope Association, a University of Oklahoma student organization built around compassion, service, education, leadership, and global citizenship.

Our Story

Created by students who believe awareness should lead to action.

GACHA began with a simple conviction: students can use their education, networks, and creativity to support vulnerable communities beyond campus. Our work focuses on African refugee children and women because access to education, healthcare, safety, and basic supplies can shape an entire future.

Mission

To improve the lives of refugee children and women through education, healthcare support, fundraising, and community partnerships.

Vision

A world where every child has access to opportunity, education, safety, dignity, and hope.

Identity

We are an OU student organization with a humanitarian mission and a growing nonprofit mindset.

Purpose

We help people understand why refugee support matters and give them a clear way to help.
