Why We Exist

Why should I care, and how can I help?

Many refugee families across Africa face barriers to education, healthcare, and basic necessities. GACHA was created by University of Oklahoma students who believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive regardless of where they were born.

The Work

What GACHA is doing now

Awareness: We host conversations, webinars, class visits, and campaigns about refugee challenges in Africa.

Fundraising: We raise support for education, hygiene, healthcare awareness, food, and basic needs.

Volunteer drives: We collect school supplies, hygiene kits, clothing, books, and approved health items.

Partnerships: We connect with student groups, local organizations, and humanitarian nonprofits.
