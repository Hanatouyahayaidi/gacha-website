What We Do

Hope, resources, and opportunity for displaced families.

GACHA is a student-led organization at the University of Oklahoma dedicated to supporting and empowering African refugees, with a special focus on children and women. We raise awareness, collect resources, and organize events that support education, healthcare, hygiene, food, and other basic needs.

Advocacy

Educate the OU community about refugee experiences and the urgent need for solidarity.

Fundraising

Raise funds for education materials, hygiene kits, food, and child-centered resources.

Partnerships

Work with NGOs, local organizations, and student groups to grow humanitarian impact.

2026 Goals

Our roadmap for impact

01

Support three refugee camps in Africa with education materials, hygiene kits, food, and child-focused resources.

02

Finalize partnerships and select two additional refugee camps to support.

03

Host three major campus awareness events about the African refugee crisis and global solidarity.

04

Partner with at least three NGOs or local organizations supporting vulnerable children and families.

05

Recruit and retain active members while building leadership succession for the next GACHA team.

Volunteer Drives

Resources we are collecting

Hygiene kits: soap, toothpaste, toothbrushes, sanitary pads, wipes, and basic care items.

Books and school materials: new or gently used children's books and educational supplies.

Clothing: clean, culturally appropriate clothing, durable shoes, school uniforms, and jackets.

Medical supplies: first-aid kits, bandages, vitamins, and approved basic health items.
